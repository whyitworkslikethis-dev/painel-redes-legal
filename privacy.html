Privacy Policy - Painel das Redes

Painel das Redes is a personal analytics dashboard that runs only on its owner's computer. After the owner authorizes access through TikTok Login Kit, it reads statistics from the owner's own TikTok account: follower count, likes, video count, and per-video views, likes, comments and shares.

This data is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server, not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings, and all stored data can be deleted by deleting the app's data folder.
